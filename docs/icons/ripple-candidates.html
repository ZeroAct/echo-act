Origin as the icon; Ieung is already hiding inside it

Take Origin's dot and its heaviest ring and discard the rest — that is Ieung. So the small form is not a second design: it is the same drawing, told the truth about 16 px. Ship Origin for the tile and lockups, and let the ladder fall to Ieung under 24 px.

Horizon earns the about screen and the installer, where the story is told in one sentence — and Caret Echo belongs to the loading state: a caret ripple animating outward while the first sentence renders. One concept, four jobs, zero extra invention.
